# Playtest checklist: 20 minutes a player

Bots test the numbers; only people test the fun. Two or three people who have never seen the game tell you more in an hour than any review: what they laugh at, where they get stuck, which lines they skip. Log each player as a row in the Playtest log in `notes/acceptance.md`.

## Before
- A fresh browser (a private window), so there is no save and no record. Sound on. A normal laptop window, not full screen.
- Start them at the notice board (`npm run dev`, the plain address). For a quick look at the ending only, `?seed=1&day=7` starts Humanity Day.
- Say only: "You're the new clerk. I won't help." Then watch. Do not explain, hint, laugh along or react; answer "what do you think?" to any question.
- Keep a timer running and note the time of everything below.

## Watch
**Day 1 (about 6 minutes)**
- The welcome letter: do they read it, and for how long?
- Pat, the second applicant: do they find INSPECT from the supervisor's note alone? Do they challenge on a hunch instead?
- The third applicant (the first robot, legal today): do they see the light in frame 3? What do they stamp, and why?
- Gordon Pim's "ministry": caught alone, or missed?
- The court at five: do they understand why a case was dismissed?

**Day 2 (about 5 minutes)**
- The paper's reprinted frame: do they connect it to yesterday's robot without being told?
- Rule 2's page and its figure: do they use it to find the light?
- The clock: rushed, bored, or right?

**Day 3 (about 5 minutes)**
- Likeness's letter: sign it or hand it in, and what do they say about it?
- The paper: do they read the headline? Anything below it?

**Humanity Day (optional, about 4 minutes, from the link above)**
- The last applicant: do they notice their own papers say "a real clerk"? What do they stamp?
- Six o'clock: which of the nine lines get a laugh, a smile, nothing? Does 0.0003 land without explanation?

## Write down, for each player
- **Laughed at:** the exact line or moment, and whether it was a laugh or a smile.
- **Confused by:** where they stopped, what they asked, what they tried that did nothing.
- **Skipped:** any text they clicked past without reading.
- **Mistakes per day:** from each evening's statement.
- **Said unprompted:** anything, word for word.

## After (2 minutes)
1. What was the game about?
2. What was the funniest moment?
3. Where were you unsure what to do?
4. Would you play tomorrow's week?

## Then
- Cut a joke nobody laughed at after three players.
- Fix anything two of three players got stuck on, in the game, not with more text.
- Never fix a confusion by explaining it in the moment: the player's first guess is the design's real feedback.
